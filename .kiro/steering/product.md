# Product Definition - InvoiceIQ

## One-Line Pitch

An AI agent that turns the pile of bills, invoices and subscription charges in someone's life into a single searchable, normalised record -- and tells them what to actually do about each one.

## Core User Pain

A new invoice arrives and the person does not know whether it is legitimate, whether they already paid it, whether the amount changed since last time, whether they still use the service, or whether they should challenge it.

## Primary User

An individual or sole trader in the UK/EU handling 10-200 invoices a year across utilities, telecoms, SaaS subscriptions, insurance, trades and councils.

## Differentiator

Every document, whatever its source format, is stored as one canonical JSON invoice record. That normalisation is what makes cross-vendor search, trend detection and AI analytics possible.

## Non-Goals for v1

The following are explicitly out of scope for the initial release:

- No bank integration
- No actual payment execution
- No accounting ledger
- No multi-tenant organisations
- No mobile app
